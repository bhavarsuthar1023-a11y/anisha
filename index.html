About Anisha Mehendi Art 🌿

Preserving tradition with a modern touch.

100% Organic

Natural Henna

With over a decade of experience, Anisha Mehendi Art beautifully blends the timeless tradition of Mehendi with modern artistry. We specialize in creating detailed, personalized designs that reflect your unique style and make every occasion truly memorable.

From bridal to festive designs, each creation is crafted with care, precision, and creativity. We use high-quality, skin-safe henna to ensure a rich, dark, and long-lasting stain for all skin types.

Celebrate your special moments with elegance, tradition, and a touch of artistry ✨
